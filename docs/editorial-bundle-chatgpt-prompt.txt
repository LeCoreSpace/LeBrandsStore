DESIGN DIRECTION
Create a professional fashion-store design inspired by editorial publishing: expressive serif headlines, restrained sans-serif body text, generous but purposeful whitespace, asymmetrical image-and-copy compositions, refined product grids and clear shopping controls.

Use Instrument Serif for headings and Instrument Sans for body text, or specify a closely matching openly licensed, self-hostable alternative if these are unavailable. Use only two font families.

Use white as the main page background, near-black text and a restrained deep burgundy accent. Do not use beige as the main background. Product photographs can retain their original backgrounds. Define accessible text and button colour combinations.

The result must look like a coherent complete store, not a collection of unrelated landing pages. Keep the header, navigation, buttons, product cards, spacing and footer consistent. Editorial does not mean oversized empty gaps, illegible tiny text or decorative elements that interfere with shopping.

Do not use sparkle symbols, sparkle icons, emojis, em dashes or en dashes in any copy. Use ordinary commas, full stops or "to". Do not add decorative floating boxes.
