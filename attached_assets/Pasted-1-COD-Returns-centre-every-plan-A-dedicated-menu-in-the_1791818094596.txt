1. COD & Returns centre (every plan)

A dedicated menu in the dashboard:

COD orders

All COD orders in one view: pending, shipped, delivered, payment collected, payment remitted by the courier.
COD verification before shipping: an automatic WhatsApp or SMS confirmation (“Confirm your order: Yes / Cancel”). Unconfirmed orders are flagged before you spend money shipping them.
Risk flags: first-time customer, high order value, pincode with high past RTO, repeated failed deliveries for this phone number.
Remittance tracking: what the courier has collected vs paid out to you.

Returns and RTO

Every return or RTO needs a reason chosen from a fixed list: customer refused, not reachable, wrong address, ordered by mistake, delivery delayed, size/fit, damaged, quality not as expected, other (with a note).
Two separate types, because they’re different problems: customer returns (after delivery) and RTO (never delivered, returned to you).
Restock decision per item (back to stock or damaged).
Cost of each RTO: forward shipping, return shipping and packaging.

RTO analysis

RTO rate overall and by pincode, state, product, order value, new vs returning customer, day of week.
Top reasons for returns and RTOs.
Money lost to RTO each month.
Practical suggestions: “Pincode 6xxxxx: 38% RTO, consider prepaid-only there”, with one click to apply that rule.

This becomes part of the core pitch: “Know exactly why every COD order came back.”
