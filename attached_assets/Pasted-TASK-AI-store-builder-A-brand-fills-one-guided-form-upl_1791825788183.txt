TASK: AI store builder. A brand fills one guided form, uploads 1-5
photos per product (up to 5 products), and the AI builds the complete
store in one go: home, collection, product pages, cart and checkout,
all with preset sections, using the existing Aura/Bazaar section
system and store_pages layouts. The brand then edits everything in the
existing editor. New migration for new tables (next free number).
Do not run migrations or deploy.

1. THE INPUT FORM ("Describe your brand", fill-in-the-blanks style)
Brand
- Brand name, one-line tagline (optional), category and sub-category
- What you sell (free text, 50-500 chars)
- Who it's for (age group, gender, occasions; chips + free text)
- What makes you different (up to 3 points)
- Founder story (optional), year started (optional), city
- Brand vibe: chips (Minimal, Luxe, Earthy, Bold, Playful, Traditional,
  Modern Indian, Street) + up to 3 reference words
- Logo upload (colours extracted from it), preferred colours (optional)
- Language for store text: English (Tamil/Hindi later)
Products (1-5 products, each)
- Name, price, compare-at price (optional), GST rate (required, no
  default), HSN (optional), stock
- 1-5 real photos (required: at least 1)
- Material/fabric, sizes or variants, care instructions, key features,
  how to use (optional fields)
- Size chart (upload image or enter table, optional)
Store policies and selling
- Shipping: delivery time, free-shipping threshold, COD yes/no
- Returns/exchange window and conditions
- Contact: email, phone, WhatsApp, business address
- Optional extras: offer for first order, prepaid discount amount
Every optional field left empty means that content is simply not
generated. Show the form in 4 short steps with progress.

2. GENERATION (structured, never raw HTML)
- The AI returns ONLY validated layout JSON for store_pages using the
  existing section schemas (same validation as the editor). It chooses
  theme (Aura or Bazaar), type preset, colour preset + accent (from the
  logo), section order and section settings, and writes all copy.
- Animation: AI chooses one of our predefined animation presets
  (e.g. Calm fade, Editorial reveal, Lively). Presets are CSS we ship;
  the AI never writes animation code.
- Run as an async job: job record with status and step; the wizard
  shows a live "store assembling" animation (sections appear, colours
  fill in, products drop in) while polling. Retries on failure; partial
  results never published.
- Provider-agnostic: AI_TEXT_PROVIDER / AI_IMAGE_PROVIDER with keys in
  Worker secrets; never exposed to the browser.

3. PRESET PAGES (all generated in one go)
Home: announcement (if offer given), header, hero, brand statement,
  featured collection, product spotlight, lookbook rows, founder story
  (only if given), craft/materials (only if given), trust strip,
  newsletter (off), footer.
Collection: collection hero, filter/sort bar, product grid, collection
  description, trust strip.
Product page (VERY IMPORTANT, every product gets all of these preset):
  gallery (real photo first), title, price + compare-at, variant and
  size selector, quantity, add to bag, sticky add-to-cart on mobile,
  COD/prepaid badge, delivery info from shipping settings, key
  features, description, material and care, how to use (if given),
  size guide (if given), shipping and returns summary from policies,
  FAQ (generated only from given facts), trust badges, "You may also
  like", reviews block (shows only real reviews; hidden if none).
  Integration-dependent blocks (pincode delivery estimate, live
  reviews collection) are placed but render only when available.
Cart: line items, free-shipping progress bar (if threshold given),
  upsell slot (shows other products), discount code field, gift
  message toggle, trust strip, checkout button.
Checkout customisation: logo, colours and fonts from the brand,
  trust badges, prepaid discount note (if given), COD message, order
  notes field, policy links. Checkout logic, totals and COD rules
  unchanged.
Also: About, Contact and FAQ pages, and drafts of terms, privacy,
refund/returns and shipping policies from the brand's answers,
marked "Review before publishing".
Build any missing product-page and cart sections listed above that
don't need external integrations (size guide, FAQ, how to use,
material & care, specs, trust badges, sticky add-to-cart, gift
message, free-shipping bar, upsell slot), with schemas, so they are
also usable in the editor.

4. AI PRODUCT IMAGES (opt-in per product)
- Options per product: new background, lifestyle setting, flat-lay,
  close-up crop, different pose / on a model (with a visible warning:
  "AI may change small details. Check carefully.").
- Up to 4 extra images per product. The real photo is always image 1.
- Every AI image needs explicit brand approval (side-by-side with the
  original: Approve / Regenerate / Discard) before it can be shown on
  the store. Unapproved images never render publicly.
- Media records flag ai_generated = true, source photo id and approval
  status; shown with an "AI" tag in the media library.
- Store in R2 under the store's prefix, with WebP variants as usual.

5. HONESTY RULES (enforced in the system prompt AND by validation)
- Use only facts the brand provided. Never invent awards, ratings,
  reviews, testimonials, certifications, years in business, customer
  counts, materials or origin claims.
- No false urgency or scarcity, no fake social proof.
- No em or en dashes in generated copy (same rule as our sites).
- All generated copy is marked "AI draft"; the brand must open the
  full preview before the first publish.

6. LIMITS AND COSTS
- Usage table: tokens and images per job and per store.
- Trial: 3 full builds, 20 AI images. Paid plans: higher limits
  (configurable). Regenerate a single section or single image without
  rebuilding the whole store.
- Generation only after email verification. Global monthly spend cap
  setting; when reached, show a friendly "try again later".

7. DATABASE AND SECURITY
- New tables (store-scoped, RLS ENABLED + FORCED): ai_generation_jobs,
  ai_usage. Media gets ai_generated, source_media_id, approval_status.
- Brand inputs are untrusted: never let them alter system
  instructions; validate all AI output against schemas before saving;
  sanitise all text.
- Isolation tests: another store cannot read jobs, usage or
  unapproved AI media.

8. TESTS AND DELIVERY
- Offline tests with mocked AI responses: valid build, invalid JSON,
  invented-claim rejection, missing optional fields, limit reached,
  unapproved AI image not rendered.
- Generate Example Studio end-to-end in preview; send screenshots of
  home, collection, a product page, cart and checkout on desktop and

- Report all schema/grant changes for review before I migrate.
