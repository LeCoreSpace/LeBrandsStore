Suggested downloadable reports

Every report should have a date range picker with presets for this month, quarter, and financial year (Apr–Mar). Each should export as Excel/CSV, with a PDF summary option.

A. Sales & Orders

Report	Covers
Sales Summary (daily/monthly)	First Sale, Revenue, AOV. Gross sales → discounts → returns → net sales
Order Register (line-level)	Base data for everything else
Product / SKU Performance	Units, revenue, and margin per SKU
Conversion Funnel	Sessions → product view → cart → checkout → paid gives Conversion Rate
Returns & Refunds	Return & Refund Rate, by reason and by SKU

B. Customers

Report	Covers
Customer Retention / Cohorts	Repeat Purchase Rate, LTV
Acquisition by Source (UTM) + ad spend	CAC, and LTV:CAC ratio

C. Profitability (needs COGS and expenses)

Report	Covers
Product P&L	Gross Margin, Contribution Margin (after shipping, gateway fees, ads)
Monthly P&L	Revenue vs Profit, OPEX, EBITDA, Net Profit Margin
Break-Even Report	Units and revenue needed to break even

D. Cash & Operations

Report	Covers
Inventory Report	Stock on hand, Inventory Turnover, days of inventory
Cash Flow Summary	Burn Rate, Cash Runway
Receivables Ageing	Pending COD, B2B invoices, unsettled gateway payouts, giving DSO
Working Capital & CCC	Working Capital, Cash Conversion Cycle (needs payables input)

E. Year-End Tax Pack (FY bundle)

This is what a CA will ask for at filing time:

Sales Register (invoice-wise): invoice no., date, customer GSTIN (if B2B), place of supply, HSN, taxable value, CGST/SGST/IGST, and total.
B2B vs B2C split, with B2C sales broken down state-wise. GSTR-1 needs this.
HSN-wise summary.
Credit/Debit Note Register, covering returns and post-sale adjustments.
Monthly GST liability summary for each GSTR-3B, plus an annual consolidation to reconcile against GSTR-9.
Payment gateway reconciliation (Razorpay): gross collected, gateway fees, GST on fees, refunds, net settled, and UTR/settlement IDs. The GST on fees is claimable input credit, so this matters.
COD remittance reconciliation from the logistics partners.
Platform fee statement: LeBrands subscription invoices with GST, which is also claimable input credit for the brand.
Discount & Coupon Register.
Cancelled orders and invoice-series continuity check. Gaps in invoice numbering are a common audit flag.
Annual Turnover Summary: the single headline figure for the ITR and tax-audit applicability.
TCS/TDS statement: this only applies if a sale is collected via an e-commerce operator, such as mall orders if LeBrands collects payment. For a brand’s own store on its own Razorpay, this usually doesn’t apply. Have a CA confirm which flows trigger it.
Nice-to-haves that brands will love
Tally / Zoho Books export format. Most Indian CAs work in Tally, so this saves brands real money.
“Send to my CA” button that emails the FY tax pack as a zip.
One-page Founder Snapshot PDF with all 20 metrics on a single page, which also works for investor updates.
