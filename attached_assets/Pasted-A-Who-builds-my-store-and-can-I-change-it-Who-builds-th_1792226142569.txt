A. “Who builds my store, and can I change it?”

Who builds the store: you alone, an agency you hire, the platform’s team, or AI guided by you (Ann). LeBrands win.
Hiring needed to launch: none, optional, or practically required (Shopify brands usually hire someone). LeBrands win.
Design control after launch: a full theme editor, limited edits, or the platform decides. LeBrands win against ShopDeck; level with Shopify.
Builder in Indian languages: Ann in Hindi, Tamil and others vs English-only dashboards. LeBrands win, once it’s built.

B. “What will it really cost me?”
5. Commission on sales: 0%, a per-order fee, or a revenue share. LeBrands win.
6. Paid extras: apps, premium themes, plugins, agency retainers. LeBrands win; you include common app features in every plan.
7. Monthly cost at your sales level, pulled from the calculator. LeBrands win against percentage-fee platforms; Zoho is cheaper.

C. “What’s included without add-ons?”
8. COD and RTO tools: COD return reasons, RTO tracking and analysis. LeBrands win.
9. Conversion features built in: reviews, upsell and cross-sell, social proof, abandoned cart. LeBrands win on paper; Shopify gets there with apps.
10. Customer messaging: order updates on Arattai plus email included, vs paid WhatsApp apps. LeBrands win, once built.
11. Integrations ready to connect: Razorpay, Shiprocket, GST check. Shopify wins on breadth; LeBrands is simpler.

D. “Who controls my business?”
12. Who owns customer data: can you see, export and call your buyers? LeBrands win against ShopDeck.
13. Whose payment and courier accounts: yours or the platform’s. LeBrands win.
14. Easy to leave: data export and domain portability. Level with Shopify.

E. “Where do customers come from?”
15. Built-in discovery: a shopper mall, nothing, or managed paid ads. LeBrands’ unique row, if you show LeBrands.Space.
16. Who runs marketing: you, or the platform’s team. ShopDeck wins for founders who want it done for them.
17. Selling in AI chats like ChatGPT. Shopify wins today.

F. “Is it built for India?”
18. India-native setup: INR pricing, GST-verified onboarding, UPI and COD as defaults. LeBrands, Zoho and Dukaan level; Wix and BigCommerce weak.
19. Support: channel, language, and response speed. Needs your actual support plan to fill.
