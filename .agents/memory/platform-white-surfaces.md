---
name: Platform white surfaces
description: User requires beige homepage and application backgrounds to become white
---
All application page backgrounds or sections that are beige should be white,
including the homepage.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this across the application, after the
pricing page was made white.

**How to apply:** Use white for platform neutral surfaces. Preserve dark
sections, accent colours, semantic warnings and statuses, and merchant-chosen
storefront presets. Theme thumbnails represent merchant presets, not platform
backgrounds.
