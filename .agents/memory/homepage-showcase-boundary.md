name: Homepage showcase boundary
description: Modern Boutique skincare homepage previews versus live stores and operational media
---

Use the existing Ann skincare sample store in Modern Boutique for the homepage
hero, Ann preview and Crafted details storefront/checkout images. Preserve the
operational dashboard and invoice visuals.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected the existing skincare Modern Boutique
design after the earlier Editorial clothing showcase.

**How to apply:** Use fictional, isolated Ann captures and fresh asset URLs.
A homepage refresh is not permission to change live merchant stores or
operational screens.

Only the homepage hero and footer remain dark; the other sections are white in
both modes. Its store, checkout, back-office and paperwork showcase belongs in
one auto-playing carousel, not four tall stacked rows.

**Why:** The later white-section request supersedes alternating bands. The user
also requested one auto carousel instead of four stacked showcase rows.

**How to apply:** Keep the preserved operational imagery, but present it together.
Use actual visible carousel movement rather than claiming static screenshots or
subtle one-time reveals demonstrate the requested animation.

Keep section headings restrained and use visible motion while sections are being
viewed, not only entrance fades that finish before the user notices them.

**Why:** The user repeatedly said the landing page looked static and its section
headings were too big.

**How to apply:** Preserve readable, stationary body copy; animate decorative
elements and media, and verify actual movement in a browser rather than relying
on static screenshots. Keep reduced-motion and offscreen/tab-hidden pauses.

Free discovery through the LeBrands.Space virtual mall is available to every
approved store. It is provided manually, not automatically.

**Why:** The user confirmed availability and reversed the earlier instruction
to remove LeBrands.Space from marketing. They explicitly said not to mention
the manual process or those setup details in public copy.

**How to apply:** Market the free discovery benefit prominently near the hero.
Do not imply automatic listing, guaranteed traffic or sales,
or restore the unsupported 300,000+ brands figure. Preserve the legal company
name LeBrandsSpace Digital Private Limited in the footer, terms and policies.

The discovery visual must come from the actual LeBrands.Space homepage, not
an invented directory of example brands.

**Why:** The user pointed to the fictional directory and requested the actual
LeBrands.Space homepage instead.

**How to apply:** Use a genuine public-homepage capture, label it as a screenshot
and link to the source. Do not present a capture as a live embedded website.

The homepage hero should have a black background in both colour modes and use
moving visual elements, text and slides to explain what the platform offers
inside the hero itself. It should not be only a clothing-store screenshot.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a black hero that explains the platform
through animated elements, referencing SVGator's website-animation examples.

**How to apply:** Keep the platform explanation readable independently of
autoplay. Use available catalogue, COD order, customer and invoice capabilities
as the story; label illustrative UI and future integrations honestly. Preserve
the separate clothing concept and all operational screens. The hero's black
background is an exception to the page's mode-dependent alternating bands.

The hero's text and complete preview screen should travel together slowly along
an oval path, left to right. The waiting scene belongs smaller and far toward the
upper-left, not directly behind or overprinted on the current headline. Keep one
short changing sentence on the left rather than explanatory paragraphs or chips.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested circular depth, then clarified an oval path
and a separate upper-left waiting scene. This replaces earlier requests for
different fly-in and fly-out effects. They also requested a transparent hero logo,
larger header menu text and the realistic 3D dashboard appearance.

**How to apply:** Preserve the visible SVG, slide and ambient motion when making
future revisions. For Razorpay and logistics, use integration setup/contact
wording instead of "Coming soon"; the user explicitly removed that wording for
those integrations. A marketing copy change does not enable backend integrations
or authorise changes to the real dashboard.

The three promises about zero commission, brand-owned domain/payments/customer
data, and not marketing to customers are prominent differentiators.
Keep them as individual animated slides following the discovery highlight.

**Why:** The user asked for each promise to have its own slide.

**How to apply:** Only the homepage hero and footer keep the black-to-plum
gradient. All intervening homepage sections are white in both modes, as requested
in the user's later white-surface direction.

The hero must lead with the fictional skincare storefront in Modern Boutique on
desktop and mobile, then show the operational dashboards. Do not retain a
separate brand-website showcase section or the hanging square decorative icons.

**Why:** The user changed direction to show the store first inside the hero and
avoid a duplicate section.

**How to apply:** Keep the store and operational sequence together, with working
controls and reduced-motion support.

Do not use "coming soon" or COD-only/current-option promotional disclaimers on
the homepage or store pages.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested their removal across these surfaces.

**How to apply:** Describe actual features without those disclaimers. This is a
copy boundary, not permission to enable unsupported payments or remove
checkout eligibility errors, disabled-feature states, or security checks.

Refresh captured wizard and dashboard media when LeBrands branding changes,
including the wizard recording, its first-frame poster and responsive formats.

**Why:** The user identified outdated green logos in these homepage captures
even after the actual application had the new hanging-sign logo and plum accents.

**How to apply:** Capture the current real UI with fictional local fixtures,
preserve merchant/storefront colours, and use fresh asset URLs to avoid stale
cached images. This does not authorise changing operational behaviour.

The homepage needs one coherent abstract pattern flowing across section
boundaries, with each section background acknowledging it.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a continuous design rather than
independent-looking sections.

**How to apply:** Keep genuinely light bands and the shared dark gradient.
Keep the pattern behind readable content and preserve the existing hero motion.
