name: Platform crawler boundary
description: Marketing versus tenant/private indexing and truthful AI-facing claims
---
Keep the platform's Google Analytics tag separate from private account/editor
pages and merchant storefronts.

**Why:** Platform visitor measurement must not silently become cross-tenant
merchant tracking or disclose operational URLs to a third party.

**How to apply:** Scope future platform measurement to public responses;
require separate scope for merchant analytics. Privacy preferences and disabled
advertising signals are not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific consent.

The root platform sitemap covers the public marketing and information pages,
not merchant storefronts, accounts, dashboards, invoices or local demonstrations.
An unfinished policy should not enter the sitemap.

**Why:** Merchant stores are separate brands with their own settings; the
platform's search data must not expose private operational or fictional sample
routes.

**How to apply:** Require separate scope before adding merchant sitemaps.
Keep account and development robots restrictive. Development previews must
preserve production URLs in crawler files rather than rewrite them locally.

AI-facing descriptions and structured data must distinguish the store builder
from the LeBrands.Space discovery service. They must not invent prices, ratings,
counts, automatic integration availability or compliance guarantees.

**Why:** The product has verified availability boundaries and pricing has not
been finalised. Machine-readable data is still a public marketing claim.

**How to apply:** Keep FAQ schema and displayed answers sourced together; use
confirmed company facts and avoid unsupported freshness dates.

Merchant blogs should be separate from the platform blog, with fillable content sections and a hero creative.

**Why:** The user described a store-specific blog where merchants fill sections and add their own hero creative.

**How to apply:** Preserve this structured merchant-authoring direction when scoping store blogs.

Store-level blogs and discovery files should be delivered in phases and apply to existing stores as well as newly created ones.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested phased implementation “even for existing 4 stores”.

**How to apply:** Avoid new-store-only initialization or requiring merchants to republish unrelated catalogue drafts to receive discovery features.

Keep merchant discovery-file contents and blog management together under “Content for LLM”, with Blog as a submenu.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested that brands see each generated file's contents in this menu and create, edit and delete blogs beneath it.

**How to apply:** Preserve this information grouping when extending merchant content tools. Keep merchant content separate from platform marketing content.
