---
name: Ann workspace layout
description: User-approved focus-adaptive split and space-saving boundaries for Ann.
---

Ann should give the preview more room when the brand works in it, leaving the
conversation visible at roughly one quarter of the workspace. Working in the
conversation restores an equal split. Keep the compact ticket style and provide
a layout lock without adding another toolbar row.

**Why:** The user approved this behaviour to address limited screen space and
avoid an editor dominated by controls.

**How to apply:** Respond to clicks and keyboard focus, not hover. Preserve
in-progress answers, scroll position and the selected preview mode. Resizing is
a workspace change, not a reason to reload the preview or save/change the theme.
On phones, keep conversation/preview switching instead of squeezed columns.
