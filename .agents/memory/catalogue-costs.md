---
name: Catalogue costs
description: COGS placement, optional form entry and historical analytics boundaries.
---

COGS belongs inside the Catalogue menu, not as a separate top-level menu.

**Why:** The user explicitly said, “cogs can be inside catalogue menu.”

**How to apply:** Use Catalogue → COGS when implementing cost management.

Costs are optional in both Ann and dashboard product forms, with existing
products also editable from Catalogue.

**Why:** The user requested optional cost entry in both product-form entry points.

**How to apply:** Keep the shared form and Catalogue workflows consistent; do
not make COGS a requirement for adding or publishing a product.

Treat unknown historical costs separately from known zero. New cost changes
are prospective, not permission to reconstruct earlier margins using today's
catalogue costs.

**Why:** The approved cost-first analytics approach must not present incomplete
cost evidence as profit or rewrite past results when a brand changes costs.

**How to apply:** Future analytics should show cost coverage, preserve captured
unit costs and distinguish gross margin from profit.
