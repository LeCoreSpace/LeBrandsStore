---
name: Platform information boundary
description: Verified company information and legal-review boundaries for platform pages
---
Use verified platform company details on its Terms, Privacy, Contact and
Support pages. Do not substitute a merchant's GST registration or address for
the platform company's details.

**Why:** The user asked for actual data; the project supplies the company name,
city and support email, but not a verified full registered-office address,
company phone number or commercial refund timetable.

**How to apply:** Ask for missing business details before adding them. Do not
invent phone numbers, response-time promises, refund periods or prices.

Terms and Privacy wording based on current operation is not legal approval.

**Why:** Populating public pages does not establish professional review or
confirm every commercial policy.

**How to apply:** Flag the need for qualified legal review before publication.
Confirm business decisions before making additional binding commitments.

Brands can set up payments, delivery and custom domains through their own dashboard. FAQ copy must not describe ordinary connection setup as contact-only or custom domains as unavailable in self-service.

**Why:** The user explicitly clarified that brands configure these connections through their dashboard.

**How to apply:** Keep public FAQ wording aligned with dashboard setup. Preserve provider verification and DNS requirements rather than promising immediate activation.
