---
name: GST choices and historical product values
description: Why legacy product rates must remain intact when updating selectable rates.
---

The user requires current choices of 0%, 5%, 18% and 40%. Previously saved
12% and 28% product values must stay intact and show a notice asking the
brand to confirm the rate. Do not silently rewrite old product rates when
editing other fields or publishing.

**Why:** The user explicitly required preserving saved product values while
updating the choices shown for new rates.

**How to apply:** Present only current slabs for a new product or a changed
rate, retain the historical value if unchanged, and give the owner an explicit
confirmation notice. The app had also allowed 0.25% and 3% previously, so
preserve those old values rather than destroying existing catalogue data.
