---
name: Subscription capacity policy
description: User-approved draft-inclusive product and SKU limits rather than unlimited drafts
---
Draft products and their SKUs must count against the store's subscription allowance. Do not offer unlimited drafts as a way around product or SKU limits. Storage checks must include uploaded files even if they are not published.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected allowing excess drafts: “they will end up creating more drafts consuming more clpoudflare storage.so restrict them to the number as per subscription”.

**How to apply:** Apply this policy to product preparation as well as publication. Keep the separate published-price and live-stock authority rules intact. Do not delete existing records simply because the store is already above its current allowance.
