An India-specific vendor URL can return globally extracted USD pricing even when its actual regional HTML contains INR. Verify the displayed currency and billing term from the regional HTML or the vendor's linked pricing data before marking an INR price as checked.

**Why:** During the comparison-page research, extracted Shopify and Zoho pages returned global prices. The actual Shopify regional HTML and Zoho's official currency-indexed pricing data supplied different Indian prices. The supplied brief also contained different Dukaan prices from the official annual offers.

**How to apply:** Do not convert global prices into alleged Indian prices or reuse a previous checked date after guessing. Keep missing regional prices unknown, distinguish annual commitments from monthly bills, and link any additional official source needed to reproduce the figures.

## Presentation

The Compare page must use a side-by-side table against the main differentiators, not paragraphs or platform cards. Remove the quiz and the entire cost calculator, not merely its monthly-cost comparison row.
Use the user's supplied differentiators as the foundation, with concise rows for missing product features. Explicitly include Ann's English, Hindi, Tamil, Marathi and Telugu conversation support, without implying storefront translation.
Use the same font, sizing and heading treatment as Pricing, including in dark mode.

**Why:** The user said no one would read the paragraphs, supplied a differentiator list, and explicitly requested removal of the quiz and cards.
They supplied the list again and specifically requested using those differentiators without monthly cost.
The user later explicitly requested removal of the calculator, an audit of missing features including the five Ann languages, and typography matching Pricing, including headers.

**How to apply:** Keep this rule scoped to Compare, not the separate Pricing page's plan cards. Reuse shared Pricing presentation rather than divergent heading rules. Put necessary assumptions and sources in compact expandable notes; distinguish implemented, draft-only, planned and unverified capabilities.
