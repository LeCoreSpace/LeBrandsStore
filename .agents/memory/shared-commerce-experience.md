---
name: Shared LeBrands commerce experience
description: Recognisable shopping standards across saved themes while retaining merchant branding and stable public links.
---

Product, cart, checkout and post-order pages should provide a recognisable, professional
LeBrands.Store shopping experience across every theme, while preserving the
merchant's logo and accent colours. Store favicons should reflect the
merchant, and product navigation should use readable, unique saved title slugs.
Previously shared product links must keep working.

**Why:** The user compared the recognisability of Shopify checkout with the
standard they want for LeBrands.Store and approved fixing defects before
implementing shared commerce layouts. They repeatedly rejected minor styling
changes to otherwise basic, theme-dominated forms as insufficient.

**How to apply:** Keep transactional interactions consistent instead of making
unrelated purchase flows per theme. Use a coherent commerce layout and typography
instead of letting theme-specific navigation and all-caps styling dominate it.
Do not add forced platform attribution or
invent badges, payment methods or delivery promises.

Verify the actual saved-theme framing on mobile, with product photography and a
populated cart, not just an unthemed fixture or empty forms.

**Why:** A neutral checkout fixture initially hid both theme-dependent colour
inheritance and a saved theme's fixed navigation covering checkout fields.
Successful payment submission alone did not establish responsive correctness.

**How to apply:** Check populated cart summaries and required fields at narrow
widths, including styles with fixed shortcut navigation. Keep purchase actions
and theme shortcuts from covering editable fields.
